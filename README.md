# Oak & Truss Roofing & Exteriors — Demo Site

Concept build by **Starling AI** · starlingagent.com · Jason Aguirre · (832) 808-6483

Formerly the RoofBuds Roofing Solutions demo. The original design is preserved on the
`roofbuds-original` branch. Repo: AdminStarlingagent/Oak-Truss · live at https://oakandtruss.com

## What's here
- `index.html` — main site. Bilingual EN/ES (Spanish-language browsers open in Spanish),
  scripted "Oakley" AI chat, lead form with A2P-ready consent, in-page Privacy Policy &
  SMS Terms (10DLC-compliant). Logo images and the wood-grain texture are embedded, so the
  page still renders if opened on its own.
- `internship.html`, `week4.html`, `week5.html` — summer internship program pages.
- `assets/` — logo files cut from the official Oak & Truss logo (transparent wordmark, full
  badge, crossed hammers), favicon, social share image, seamless wood-grain tile, and
  self-hosted fonts (Big Shoulders Display, Red Hat Text — SIL Open Font License).
